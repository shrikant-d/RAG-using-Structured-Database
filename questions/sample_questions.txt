Sample questions used in the thesis, grouped by database.
Pick the database named in each heading in the UI, then paste a question.
Gold SQL for every question is in the matching JSON file under questions/.
Difficulty tiers follow Spider's taxonomy: easy, medium, hard, extra.

[1] (easy) For every combination of return flag and line status, report the total quantity, total extended price, total discounted price, total discounted price plus tax, average quantity, average extended price, average discount, and number of line items, for all line items shipped on or before 90 days before 1998-12-01. [2] (hard) Among size-15 parts whose type contains 'BRASS', supplied by suppliers in Europe, find the one supplied at the lowest supply cost. [3] (medium) For customers in the 'BUILDING' market segment, find the 10 orders placed before 1995-03-15 that have at least one line item shipped after 1995-03-15, with the highest unshipped revenue (sum of extended price times one minus discount). [4] (medium) For orders placed in the third quarter of 1993 (1993-07-01 through 1993-09-30) that have at least one line item received later than its committed date, count the orders by order priority, ordered by order priority.
[5] (hard) For orders placed in 1994 by customers in the Asia region, where the supplier of each line item is in the same nation as the customer, report total revenue (extended price times one minus discount) by nation, ordered by revenue descending.
[6] (easy) What is the total revenue lost (sum of extended price times discount) from line items shipped during 1994 with a discount between 0.05 and 0.07 inclusive and quantity less than 24?
[7] (hard) For shipments between France and Germany (in either direction) with a ship date in 1995 or 1996, report total shipping revenue (extended price times one minus discount) broken down by supplier nation, customer nation, and shipment year, ordered by supplier nation, customer nation, then year.
[8] (extra) Among orders placed in 1995 or 1996 by customers in the Americas for parts of type 'ECONOMY ANODIZED STEEL', what fraction of total revenue in each order year came from suppliers based in the United States? Report order year and market share, ordered by year.
[9] (hard) For parts whose name contains 'green', report total profit (revenue minus supply cost, across every line item, order, and supplier involved) broken down by supplier nation and order year, ordered by nation then year descending.
[10] (medium) For orders placed in the fourth quarter of 1993 (1993-10-01 through 1993-12-31) that included at least one returned line item, report the top 20 customers by lost revenue (sum of extended price times one minus discount on the returned lines): customer key, name, revenue, account balance, nation, address, phone, and comment.
[11] (hard) Among parts stocked by suppliers in Germany, find every part whose total stock value (supply cost times available quantity, summed over German suppliers of that part) exceeds 0.01% of the combined stock value of all parts stocked by German suppliers. Report part key and stock value, ordered by stock value descending.
[12] (medium) For line items shipped by mail or by ship, where the ship date is before the commit date and the commit date is before the receipt date, and the receipt date falls in 1994, report for each shipping mode the number of associated orders with high priority ('1-URGENT' or '2-HIGH') versus other priorities, ordered by shipping mode.
[13] (hard) For every customer, count how many orders they have placed whose comment does not contain the word 'special' followed anywhere later by the word 'requests' (customers with no such orders count as zero). Then report, for each order count, how many customers have that count, ordered by number of customers descending, then by order count descending.
[14] (easy) Of all revenue (extended price times one minus discount) from line items shipped during September 1995, what percentage came from parts whose type starts with 'PROMO'?
[15] (hard) Considering only line items shipped in the first quarter of 1996 (1996-01-01 through 1996-03-31), which supplier generated the most total revenue (extended price times one minus discount)? Report supplier key, name, address, phone, and their total revenue.
[16] (hard) For parts that are not brand 'Brand#45', whose type does not start with 'MEDIUM POLISHED', whose size is one of 49, 14, 23, 45, 19, 3, 36, or 9, and that are not stocked by any supplier whose comment mentions 'Customer Complaints', count the number of distinct suppliers per brand/type/size combination. Report brand, type, size, and supplier count, ordered by supplier count descending, then brand, type, size.
[17] (medium) For parts of brand 'Brand#23' packaged as 'SM PACK', what is the average yearly revenue (extended price) lost by only ever ordering less than 20% of that part's own average order quantity per line item? Assume 7 years of history.
[18] (medium) Find orders whose line items together total more than 300 units in quantity. Report customer name, customer key, order key, order date, order total price, and the summed quantity, ordered by total price descending then order date, limited to the top 100.
[19] (hard) What is the total revenue (extended price times one minus discount) from line items delivered in person by air or registered air, where each line item matches one of three brand/container/quantity/size combinations: Brand#12 in a small package (SM CASE, SM BOX, SM PACK, or SM PKG) with quantity 1-11 and part size 1-5; Brand#23 in a medium package (MED BAG, MED BOX, MED PKG, or MED PACK) with quantity 10-20 and part size 1-10; or Brand#34 in a large package (LG CASE, LG BOX, LG PACK, or LG PKG) with quantity 20-30 and part size 1-15?
[20] (hard) Which suppliers in Canada stock a part whose name starts with 'forest' in a quantity greater than half the total amount of that exact part they shipped to that exact part/supplier combination during 1994? Report supplier name and address, ordered by name.
[21] (extra) For suppliers in Saudi Arabia, find suppliers who were the only late supplier (receipt date after commit date) on a multi-supplier order that was fully shipped ('F'). Report supplier name and the number of such orders, ordered by that count descending, then by supplier name.
[22] (medium) Among customers whose phone number's first two digits are one of '13', '31', '23', '29', '30', '18', or '17', who have never placed an order, and whose account balance is above the average positive account balance of customers in that same phone-prefix set, report the count of such customers and their total account balance, grouped by phone-number country code, ordered by country code.
